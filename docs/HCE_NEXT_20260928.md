HCE search and eval, plus identical-result speedups. All matches: 10+0.1 real
clocks, Threads=1, 100 positions played with both colors (200 games),
`src/core/bot/test_lab.py`, 4 games at a time on the shared Mac (other

## Eval: distillation from Stockfish

Method (all scripts in `scripts/`, data under `current/sf_distill/`):

1. Sample up to 9 positions per game from the 8,004 self-play games of the
   August refit (`selfplay_r1.pgn`), not in check, deduplicated: 65,161.
2. Keep positions where |static - quiescence| < 50 (the tunedump rule):
   46,844.
3. Stockfish 18, depth 8, one thread: label = 1/(1+10^(-cp/250)) for White
   (`sf_winprob_labels.py`).
4. `tunedump` (labels now printed with four decimals), exact reconstruction
   verified (0 mismatches), fit with `texel_tune_lbfgs.py` (L-BFGS over the
   same 789 parameters as `texel_tune.py`, pawn fixed at 100).
5. First commit (`eab7df0`): those 43,370 positions, l2 0.05. Final commit
   (`513aa2e`): 100,490 positions (plus 44,448 from other plies of the same
   games and 12,672 from VidBot's Lichess games and this session's
   matches), l2 0.002. The dump for fitting is always made with the old
   weights (the tuner's reference vector), so both fits start from and are
   regularized toward the pre-session eval.

Why it works: the regression of Stockfish scores on the eval's own terms
showed the self-play fit had king danger, mobility, rook files and pawn
activity too small relative to material and no bishop pair; the game-result
labels are too noisy (and l2=3 too strong) to move rarely-active terms. In
probability space the first refit cuts validation loss 0.0256 -> 0.0219,
and on 12,672 positions from another source (VidBot Lichess games, this
session's matches) 0.0333 -> 0.0262. Lower l2 generalizes better on that
held-out source (fit without it): 0.0244 at l2 0.005, 0.0240 at l2 0.002
with the extra self-play positions; the games agreed (+31, then +17).

Final weights, notable values (old in brackets): king danger scale mg/eg
-119/-236 [-94/-99]; safe checks N/B/R/Q mg -58/-22/-58/-36 [0/0/0/-1];
bishop pair +53/+55 [0/0]; pawn attacking a minor +61 mg [+1]; free pawn
pushes +18/+11 [+4/+3]; rook on open file mg/eg +59/-12 [+19/+12];
hanging mg -22 [-84], queen trap mg -14 [-75] (quiescence resolves those
captures; the fit only sees quiet positions); passed-pawn scales -23/+88
[97/114] with the per-rank terms now rising steeply on ranks 6-7; castled
king squares c1/g1 +47/+43 in the middlegame king table.

What did not survive the check: a linear regression in centipawns suggested
large material-imbalance errors (exchange, minor vs pawns), but fitting
imbalance terms in probability space gains only 1% of the loss with mixed
signs; the centipawn pattern came from Stockfish's nonlinear scale. Tempo
18 instead of 12 and a 0.8 scale on the passer extras each gain under 1%.

## Search switches

Nine UCI switches (`7a8d9b1`), each off by default and each reproducing the
previous search exactly when off (identical best moves and node counts on an
80-position fixed-depth bench): HceNmpEval, HceIIR, HceImproving,
HceFutility, HceSeePrune, HceSeeOrder, HceSingular, HceLmrPv, HceRepFix.

All nine together lost 63 Elo. Most of that is HceRepFix: with it (a
repetition of a pre-root position needs a third occurrence, as in
Stockfish), 91 of 200 games ended in threefold repetition instead of 51, and
at the first repetition the candidate stood at +1.5 or better by Stockfish
in 31 of them (1 of 51 without it; 0 of 39 in the pruning-subset match). The
engine's rule that any single repetition scores 0 lets the better side see
and avoid a repetition four plies earlier; with the textbook rule a
perpetual is only recognized after two cycles, which pruning often misses.
Keep the existing rule.

The node-reducing subset (null move only above beta with eval-scaled R, IIR,
futility, SEE pruning) searches 32% fewer nodes to depth 12 but only saves
10% time. It was neutral with the old eval (+5 [-36, +46]) and lost with the
new one (-44 [-82, -7]); about -19 over the 400 games. Keep all four off. HceImproving, HceSeeOrder, HceSingular and HceLmrPv were not
screened individually; SeeOrder and LmrPv grow the tree (+29%, +22% nodes)
because this engine's LMR/LMP count every searched move, so moving losing
captures behind the quiets reduces quiet moves less.

## Identical-result speedups

Bench: 80 positions (45 openings/middlegames, 25 endgames, 10 fixed),
fixed depth, same process sequence, best move and node count per position
compared; speed as nodes per CPU second of the engine process (alternating
runs, the Mac was loaded). Perft unchanged on the six standard positions.

- King moves: legal iff the target is not attacked with the king removed
  from the occupancy (was make/undo per king move).
- Pinned pieces as a bitboard instead of clearing a 64-entry array per
  generation; Zobrist accessors inlined; engine init moved out of line.
- Prefetch the child's eval-cache slot next to its TT slot.

Together about +14% (1931 -> 2208 knps on this machine), +15% with the pawn-cache fix below. Tried and dropped
(no gain): an incremental SEE (identical on 27M calls but not faster), a
larger eval cache (slower), -O3 for the search file, LTO (3% slower).

Also: a 21% miss rate in the pawn cache came from indexing by the low bits
of a multiplicative hash (they only see ranks 1-2 and 5-6); indexing by the
high bits cuts it to 4% (`adb411a`, identical results, about +1%).

## Recommended package

`hce-next` at HEAD with every Hce* search switch at its default (off) and
HceQsearchTT off: the identical-result speedups plus the Stockfish-distilled
weights (`513aa2e`). Versus the live-equivalent `hce-endgame` 3e58785:
+149 [+107, +197] on openings at 30+0.3 and +54 [+29, +80] on the endgame
suite at 10+0.1 (200 games each). Deploy with the usual
`scripts/deploy_hce_release.sh`; nothing in the bot changes.

## Weaknesses found, not fixed

1. Repetition: the rule that works (any single repetition scores 0) is not
   the textbook one; the textbook rule lost games by drawing won positions
   (above). A correct version needs upcoming-repetition detection (Stockfish's
   cuckoo tables) so perpetuals are seen early.
2. LMR and LMP index every searched move, captures included, so any change
   to move order or to what gets pruned shifts the reduction schedule. The
   standard pruning additions are neutral here; they would need LMR/LMP
   retuned with them (e.g. index quiet moves only).
3. The static eval swings with the side to move: 1.e4 scores +83 to +103
   (mobility and PST) against about +30 for Stockfish. Fitting tempo on the
   Stockfish data prefers about 18 (or 30 mg / 8 eg) instead of 12, but the
   loss gain is under 1%; untested in games.
4. Hand-written terms outside the tuner (HcePasser extras, HceScale,
   HceKsFade, king-safety shape) are not refitted; the Stockfish data says
   the passer extras are about 20% too large at the extremes (0.4% loss).
5. Timed-out iterations are thrown away even when the first root move has
   been searched and another move has beaten it; with `go movetime` (the live
   bot) no iteration starts after 55% of the budget.
6. The TT is one entry per slot, always replaced by a different position;
   not a problem at 16-64 MB and these node counts, but no depth-preferred
   slot.
7. Single-pawn endings without tablebases are still over-scored (KPK
   +1.2); live has Syzygy 3-4-5.

## Reproduce

```sh
make -C src/core/engine
python3 scripts/sf_winprob_labels.py --fens quiet.fen --out labels.txt --depth 8
printf 'tunedump labels.txt feats.txt\nquit\n' | src/core/engine/chess_uci
python3 scripts/texel_current_defaults.py > cur.txt
VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=1 python3 scripts/texel_tune_lbfgs.py \
  --feats feats.txt --initial-tuned-file cur.txt --l2 0.002 > tuned.txt
python3 scripts/texel_apply_tune.py --tuned-file tuned.txt
```
